# Manually Operated Plastic Waste Shredder & Melter

A manually operated mechanical system designed to shred and melt plastic waste, converting it into raw material for the production of affordable paver blocks.

## 📌 Overview
This project addresses two problems simultaneously: local plastic waste management and the high cost of construction materials. The goal was to design a low-cost, portable, manually operated machine that could be built with locally available materials and skills.

## 🎯 The Challenge
In many developing regions, plastic waste accumulates without efficient recycling infrastructure, while affordable construction materials remain scarce. This machine allows communities to process plastic waste locally and turn it into valuable building materials.

## 🛠️ My Role
- **Conceptual Design:** Developed the initial mechanism for shredding and melting.
- **3D Modeling:** Modeled all mechanical components (shredder blades, frame, melting chamber, drive/lever mechanism) in **SolidWorks**.
- **Engineering Calculations:** Performed hand calculations to size the shredding blades, drive linkage, and frame for the required cutting force and structural loads.
- **Drafting:** Produced manufacturing and assembly drawings in **AutoCAD**.
- **Fabrication & Testing:** Built and tested a working prototype.

## 📐 Design & Analysis
The design process involved:
1.  **Force Analysis:** Calculating the required torque and cutting force to shred common plastics (PET, HDPE).
2.  **Shaft Design:** Sizing the shafts to withstand torsional and bending loads without failure.
3.  **Frame Analysis:** Ensuring the A36 mild steel frame could support the dynamic loads of the shredding process.
4.  **BOM Creation:** Generating a complete Bill of Materials for cost-effective procurement.

## 🏗️ Fabrication & Assembly
The prototype was fabricated using standard workshop processes:
- **Cutting & Welding:** The main frame and hopper were cut and welded from mild steel square tubing and plates.
- **Machining:** The shafts and bearing housings were machined to fit precisely.
- **Assembly:** The cutting discs, shafts, bearings, and hopper were assembled onto the frame.
- **Finishing:** The unit was painted to prevent corrosion.

## 📊 Results
- Successfully built and tested a working prototype.
- The machine effectively shreds plastic waste into smaller flakes suitable for melting.
- The design is cost-effective, portable, and can be manufactured locally.
